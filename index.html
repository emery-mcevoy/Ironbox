Version 1.0 for Ironbox v1.0

Ironbox is a customizable app for playing Ironsworn and other games based on it.

I made Ironbox after looking at other Ironsworn VTTs online and finding that most of them focused on a character's vows in their UI. This app is instead moves-oriented, with your move options are presented directly to you on the main screen. The goal of this design is to make the play experience as smooth as possible. It is made with an emphasis on journaling and theatre-of-mind play.

When playing the game, you can hold a move option to review it, and tap it to start a move. The app will then ask you to make your move selections and walk you through the move. If you have set an active chapter in the journal section using the flag icon, you will also be able to immediately journal the result of your move without navigating any menus.

The moves system automates some of the game's mechanics, but not all of them. The features that are automated when you roll are:

  • Which dice to roll and in what way
  • Core stat modifiers
  • Asset effects (when enabled)
  • Momentum reset

Essentially, the math is built in, while marking progress and other character content is left to the player.

"Content Packs" in the main menu is the customization feature. Use content packs to import programs that interact with the app. They can add, delete, and change anything about Ironbox to fit the game you are looking to play. Content packs can be toggled on and off in the menu.

You may notice that Ironbox is relentlessly vibe-coded. I take no pride in that fact. I just wanted a game engine. If you don’t want to use it for this or any other reason, you do you.

Please enjoy Ironbox!
